Old tint removal

$25 a door and $150 for the rear window, in most cases. We steam the old film off to protect the rear defroster. It survives most removals, but we can't guarantee it.

How dark can you go?

We carry film from 70%, the lightest, to 5%, the darkest. Darkness doesn't change the price, except 5% limo tint, which is illegal in most situations. Florida tint laws

Changing your appointment

Give us 24 hours' notice to cancel or move it. With less notice, or if you arrive more than 15 minutes late, a $50 rescheduling fee applies, because we may not be able to fill your slot.

Gift certificates

Tint makes a good gift. Pick one up at any shop, or call and we'll email it. How they work

Cut by computer, fitted by hand.

XPEL's software holds the window patterns for your exact vehicle. Our plotter cuts the film to those patterns, which gives the cleanest fit on most cars. A few vehicles still fit better with a hand cut, so we keep doing those too.

Then we install in a clean, enclosed, climate-controlled bay, one vehicle at a time.
