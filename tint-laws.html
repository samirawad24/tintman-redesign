Florida tint laws, in plain numbers

The percentage is how much light gets through the glass and film together. A lower number means darker glass. We carry film from 70% to 5%.

Factory glass counts

Your windows already carry a slight tint from the factory. An officer's meter reads the glass and the film together, so the film you choose has to leave room for it.

The windshield

Florida allows tint above the AS-1 line, the mark near the top of the glass. Film below that line is illegal without a tint waiver.

Tint waivers

The state grants waivers for medical reasons such as skin cancer or lupus. A doctor signs the form. Florida's waiver form (PDF)

The door sticker

Florida requires a compliance sticker on the inside of the driver's door jamb.

Fines

Police can write a ticket of up to $117 per window for illegal tint.

Banned film

Mirrored and colored tints are illegal in Florida.

You follow the law of the state where your vehicle is registered. This page is a summary, not legal advice. Florida Statutes 316.2951 to 316.2957 hold the full rules.
